# Lowlight privacy policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Lowlight does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app saves its settings on the user's device: whether dimming is enabled, the selected dim level, and any configured schedule times and weekdays. These settings never leave the device. Lowlight has no account system, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service, or internet permission.

Lowlight asks Android for permission to display over other apps. It uses that permission only to place a non-interactive, transparent screen shade over ordinary app windows. The shade does not receive touches, read screen content, capture images, inspect other apps, or control other apps.

Lowlight may ask for notification permission so it can show an ongoing dimming notification with a stop action or a schedule reminder. Its optional Quick Settings tile only turns dimming on and off. These notifications contain only the selected dim level or a prompt to start dimming.

Lowlight may ask for Android's Alarms & reminders access so it can apply a schedule at the exact local times chosen by the user. Alarm events remain entirely on the device and contain no personal data.

Questions about this policy can be asked on Lowlight's issue tracker: https://github.com/ctjerm/lowlight/issues
