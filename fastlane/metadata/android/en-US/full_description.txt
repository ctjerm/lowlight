Lowlight adds a simple screen shade for reading, watching, and winding down when Android's lowest brightness still feels too bright.

Set the dim level, switch it on, and keep using your phone. The shade does not intercept touches or keyboard input. A persistent notification shows that the shade is active and gives you a quick way to stop it.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Shade strength from 5% to 75%, adjustable while dimming</li>
<li>Quick Settings tile to turn dimming on and off from the notification panel</li>
<li>Optional weekly schedule that starts and stops the shade at your chosen times</li>
</ul>

<b>Permissions</b>
<ul>
<li>Display over other apps: draws the shade. Lowlight cannot read or control what is underneath.</li>
<li>Notifications: shows the active-shade notification with its stop button, and schedule reminders.</li>
<li>Alarms &amp; reminders (optional): starts a scheduled shade at the exact time. Without it, Lowlight sends a one-tap reminder instead.</li>
</ul>

Lowlight contains no ads, analytics, accounts, or internet access. Your settings and schedule stay on your device.

Android keeps critical system panels and the keyboard above app overlays, so those areas may stay brighter.
